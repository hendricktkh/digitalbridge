WHY DIGITAL BRIDGE EXISTS

Being connected is
only the beginning.

Knowing how to organise a document, attach a file or understand a spreadsheet can make everyday tasks easier. We built Digital Bridge to give learners a straightforward place to practise.

This student-led project supports the spirit of SDG 4: quality education. Its lessons introduce ICT activities associated with Indicator 4.4.1; they do not provide an official assessment.
